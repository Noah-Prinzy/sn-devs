# About page brief (round 2)

Kaiser, 2026-11-17. Written before any new mock-ups. Round 1 is in `about-mockups.html/.png`; round 2 is `about-mockups-v2.html/.png`.

## a. The page's job

- **Who lands here:** Ugandan and East African business owners, organisations and creators. They come from word of mouth or social media, as often on a phone as on a laptop. They have usually seen Home or Services and now want to know **who they would be working with**.
- **What they must leave knowing:** S&N Devs is two people, Shaun (design and creative) and Noah (engineering), working from Kampala. They build the site **and keep it running**. The price is fixed before any work starts, and you talk to the people who build it.
- **What they must leave feeling:** these two are real, careful and calm, and easy to reach.
- **The one action:** go to `/contact`.

- **Shaun:** co-founder; leads design and the creative studio (UI, video, presentations, ad creative). Email: shaundaniel156@gmail.com.

## c. Constraints

- **Motion:** "prevalent but subtle and very guiding, reduce chaos." There is one clear motion at a time and it leads the eye to the next thing. No swarms and nothing busy. Reduced motion is honoured. Use motion/react only, no WebGL.
- **Type:** rare, characterful, free fonts (Google Fonts). A set per direction is fine.
- **Theme:** near-black #0a0a0a, one green accent #4ade80 (dark text on green is #04130a), square corners.
- **Images:** stock photos only, free licence and **no people**. Shaun and Noah's real portraits are still to come, so mock-ups use the existing placeholders or a marked "your photo here" frame.
- **Navbar:** links plus "Get in touch". The pause button is gone, so don't draw it.

## d. Rejected so far, and why

- **Contact's "SAY HELLO." with chat bubbles:** chaotic, and the big word copied the Services opener.
- **Round 1 (two desks, two lines, pin on Kampala, studio file):** no reason was given. I read it as "not distinctive enough":
  - Two desks was a third dark-desk photo; Home and Contact already use one.
  - Two lines and the Kampala pin repeat the line and map motifs.
  - The studio file read like a form.
- **So for round 2:** no desk photo, no guide line, no map, no form, and no giant single word.

## e. What the other openers already do (About must not repeat these)

- **Home:** big S&N letters filled with a code photo, then a night-desk photo behind three tilted "doors" (Build, Care, Create).
- **Services:** the word SERVICES pinned over a photo that moves from a corridor to a circuit board, with floating tech-icon bubbles.
- **Contact:** a calm dark desk photo at dusk, headline left, a big live Kampala clock right.
- **About today:** Shaun's 3D "portal" walk-through with glass bubbles. It is being replaced.

## f. How I judge ideas

1. **Distinct:** you could tell it is the About page from one screenshot, and it shares no device with Home, Services or Contact.
2. **Calm guide:** one motion at a time, and that motion points to the next thing to read.
3. **About people and promises:** the opener's idea itself says "two people who build it and keep it running", not just a nice picture.
4. **Buildable and honest:** motion/react with no WebGL, works on a phone, works with placeholder portraits, needs at most two stock photos with no people.

## Step 2: ten one-line concepts

1. **The site that builds itself:** one browser window in the middle of a black page assembles from wireframe (Shaun's half) to live code (Noah's half), then shows "still running" with a care log. *Chosen.*
2. **Departures board:** a split-flap board reading KAMPALA. Each row flips over calmly, one at a time, to show who does what and the promises. *Chosen.*
3. **Diptych:** two still-life photos, one per founder (design tools and hardware), with a seam between them. Scrolling closes the seam into one finished thing. *Chosen.*
4. **End credits:** the page is a film's closing credits. It opens on a title card, and the scroll rolls "Design: Shaun", "Engineering: Noah", the promises and "Next: your project". *Chosen.*
5. **S & N, with you as the "&":** the logo's letters part to reveal the two founders, and the ampersand becomes "& you". *Dropped:* Home's opener is already the S&N letters.
6. **Instant prints:** two blank photo prints on a dark surface lift and flip to show handwritten notes. *Dropped:* close to Home's sticky-note board, and it leans hardest on portraits we don't have yet.
7. **In numbers:** giant pinned numerals (2 people, 9 services, 1 working day). *Dropped:* it is the big-word opener again, with digits.
8. **Interview transcript:** a magazine-style Q&A with Shaun and Noah. *Dropped:* it reads like chat at a glance, too near the rejected bubbles.
9. **Studio floor plan:** a top-down architectural drawing of the studio that scrolls room by room. *Dropped:* another line or map motif.
10. **Lights coming on:** the page warms from black to dawn as you scroll ("always on"). *Dropped:* it fights the dark theme, and the metaphor is weaker than (1).
