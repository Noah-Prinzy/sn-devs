Departures board

A split-flap board, the kind at a station, fills the opener. It reads the headline in tiles, with one row still mid-flip.

Scrolling turns it into a departures list: where projects go and how long they take (launch page about a week, website two to three). One row flips at a time, top to bottom, with a quiet click, so the eye just reads down. The founders are the "crew", the promises flip to CONFIRMED, and the close reads "Next departure: your project · BOARDING".
